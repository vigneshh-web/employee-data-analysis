Employee Data Analysis

  1. Problem Statement

The goal of this project is to analyse employee data to understand salary patterns, differences between departments, and the relationship between employee experience and salary.

  1. Dataset

The dataset contains 1,470 employee records after removing duplicate rows and cleaning missing values. The analysis uses employee details such as department, salary, and years of experience.

  1. Key Visualisations

Bar chart: Average salary by department.

Histogram: Distribution of employee salaries.

Scatter plot: Relationship between salary and experience.

  1. Key Insights

The charts help compare average salaries across departments, understand salary distribution, and explore how experience relates to salary.

  1. Recommendations

Review salary differences across departments.

Use experience and salary patterns to support employee compensation planning.

  1. Limitations

The analysis is based on the available employee dataset. The findings may not represent all employees or organisations.
